Food Squishy RNG is a simple luck-based game where you roll for different food squishies and try to collect them all 🎰.
To play, press the **ROLL** button and see which food and rarity you get, then collect and equip your favourites.


CHANGELOG:
**V1** started as a simple game with just 15 food squishies and basic rolling and collecting.
**V2** expanded the game with 50 foods, Shiny and Golden variants, Luck upgrades, quests, achievements, daily rewards, streaks, and more. ✨
**V3** takes it even further with 167 unique foods, new rarities including Secret, special animations, Auto-Roll, and a much bigger collection system.
